Marine Zorea is a designer and researcher exploring the emergent and critical realities of everyday technology. Guided by philosophy of technology and participatory design, she examines the mutual becomings of humans, machines, and beyond through objects, sound, images, and words. Marine received her BA in Psychology from Tel Aviv Uni. and an MSc and PhD from Kyoto Design Lab, where she examined domestic devices and their ethical articulations in a post-human era. She has collaborated with Japanese tech leaders, presented her work internationally, and published her research in top HCI publications. Currently, she leads the Masters diploma project in interaction design at Bezalel Academy of Art and Design.
